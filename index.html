01
Applied portfolio research

Constrained portfolio construction

Financial advisory internship · Fideuram - Intesa Sanpaolo Private Banking · June-September 2026

How can investment alternatives be compared under explicit allocation, concentration and risk constraints?

A Python workflow for ETF and multi-asset screening, common-window risk and correlation analysis, and constrained allocation search. The saved optimization run enumerates 65,807 admissible candidate allocations on a 2.5% weight grid, with selection on the 2019-2024 design period and separate checks on 2025-2026 data.

PythonPortfolio analyticsRisk metricsOut-of-sample checks
Evidence and scope +

Evidence

Saved run manifests and methodology document the candidate count, allocation rules and separation between design and out-of-sample evaluation. Outputs support a transparent comparison of historical alternatives.

What I take from it

Portfolio analytics is useful when the assumptions, constraints and selection process can be explained alongside the metrics.

Limits

65,807 counts candidate allocations, not managed portfolios or client outcomes. Operational trading costs are excluded, the holdout is short, and market proxies are distinct from product returns. Historical comparisons do not predict future returns or guarantee drawdown limits. Internal files and holdings are excluded.

Structuring an investment workflow, comparing risk-adjusted alternatives and translating quantitative analysis into a decision process.

Reproducible public demo · synthetic data

An independent four-asset workflow with explicit constraints, design-only selection, fees and drawdown from initial capital. Eleven unit tests and the example passed in a clean Windows/Python 3.12 environment. Original internship code and data are withheld. The README provides setup, test and run commands.

02
Master's thesis

Market regimes and robust hedging

Quantitative Finance and Risk Management · Ca' Foscari University of Venice · 2026

Do neural hedging policies remain robust when market conditions differ from their development environment?

Agent-based market simulation, neural hedging in PyTorch and comparison with a same-window Black-Scholes benchmark under transaction costs. The research includes fresh out-of-distribution stress scenarios and a causal transfer study on 139 historical SPY windows.

PyTorchAgent-based modellingExpected ShortfallModel validation
Evidence and scope +

Evidence

The recorded full studies include 100 fresh stress paths across ten seeds per neural family, and an evaluation-only closure on 139 historical SPY windows. The SPY study uses synthetic options on observed underlying-price paths, not historical traded-option quotes.

What I take from it

Development-sample gains did not establish robust aggregate superiority. Black-Scholes remains the recorded winner on the historical SPY sample; negative findings are retained.

Limits

The historical SPY sample is already inspected and cannot be reused as an untouched test or for model selection. Simulated hedging results do not establish investment returns or execution feasibility.

Benchmark design, chronological data discipline, stress testing and communicating unfavourable evidence without selecting convenient runs.

Reproducible reduced thesis experiment · synthetic data

Preserved ABM and causal delta-hedging components, two synthetic scenarios and eight overlapping option windows. Eight tests and the numerical-reference comparison passed in a clean Windows/Python 3.12 environment. Neural training and weights are excluded; this does not replicate the full thesis. Tail-risk outputs are descriptive.

03
Continuing research

Real-options research infrastructure

Independent continuation of the thesis · active development · November 2026

What infrastructure is needed to test a hedge using option-market data, explicit execution assumptions and a recoverable financial account?

An event-driven Python laboratory connecting market-data provenance, causal observations, conservative liquidity scenarios, option and futures accounting, and transactional SQLite recovery. Recurrent policy prototypes connect observations, memory and financial state.

Event-driven PythonSQLiteDeterministic recoveryData provenance
Evidence and scope +

Evidence

The frozen offline RO-01 foundation records 59 passing tests and twelve engineering gates. Later synthetic pilots verify complete option/futures round trips and independent cash reconciliation. A separate continuity exercise preserves state across 130 observations and 17 recovery segments.

What I take from it

This work makes research decisions and account changes inspectable, including partial fills, uncertain acknowledgements, invalid data and interrupted execution.

Limits

The current recurrent actors are untrained. The long-horizon exercise passed its functional checks but failed its CPU budget; it does not close a full financial horizon. Qualified market data, complete learners and economic validation remain open. No live trading or demonstrated profitability.

Designing auditable state transitions, exact accounting, failure tests and reproducible evidence while preserving operational limits.

Reproducible RO-01 foundation · offline fixtures

The original 59-test foundation plus five new demo checks and a synthetic accounting example passed in a clean Windows/Python 3.12 environment. Inspect L2 replay, the exact ledger and JSONL recovery. Later SQLite and recurrent-model extensions are outside this public subset; economic validation remains open.
